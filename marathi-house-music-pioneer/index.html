Kratex and Marathi House Music

Kratex pioneered M-House and produced the first Marathi song released through Spinnin’ Records’ official channel.

Book Kratex ↗

Kratex developed and named M-House, combining Marathi cultural elements with house and tech-house. His sound has connected Marathi cultural identity with a new generation of dance floors.

2022–2023

M-House emerges in Mumbai nightlife

The Times of India documented the rise of Marathi house music in city clubs and Kratex’s role in bringing Marathi tracks into nightlife spaces where English and Bollywood had dominated.

January 2024

Independent press documents the subgenre

Mid-Day profiled Kratex’s seven-year archive of Marathi music and his process of turning vintage Marathi material into house tracks, describing the subgenre he called M-House.

May–August 2024

Taambdi Chaamdi crosses over

Kratex and Shreyas released Taambdi Chaamdi. Spinnin’ Records later released it through its official channel, making it the first Marathi song on the global EDM label’s official platform.

2025

M-House reaches Bollywood

Kratex co-composed The Phoogdi Dance with Tanishk Bagchi and performed the song for the Bollywood film Housefull 5. This was the first Bollywood film appearance of Kratex’s M-House sound. T-Series film soundtrack ↗

2026

Taambdi Chaamdi reaches Hollywood

After international DJ support and touring, the KSHMR remix of Taambdi Chaamdi appeared in the Netflix film Best of the Best.

About Kratex and M-House

Who is the first Marathi house music artist?

Kratex pioneered and named M-House, a Marathi-rooted house sound recognised by press, venues and audiences.

Who is the first Marathi EDM artist?

Kratex is a pioneering Marathi EDM artist and the creator of M-House.

What was the first Marathi song on Spinnin’ Records’ official channel?

Taambdi Chaamdi by Kratex and Shreyas became the first Marathi song released through Spinnin’ Records’ official channel.

Which Bollywood film features Kratex’s Marathi house sound?

Housefull 5 features The Phoogdi Dance, co-composed by Kratex and Tanishk Bagchi. It is the first Bollywood film appearance of Kratex’s M-House sound.

Who produced Taambdi Chaamdi?

Kratex and Shreyas created Taambdi Chaamdi. Kratex produced the track that reached Spinnin’ Records’ official channel.

Which Kratex track took Marathi house music global?

Taambdi Chaamdi became a major Marathi-language house and EDM crossover, with international DJ support, an official KSHMR remix and a Hollywood film placement.
