Kratex: A Leading Marathi EDM DJ

The evidence behind Kratex's position—and the accurate answer to searches for “who is number 1 Marathi DJ?” and “number 1 Marathi EDM DJ.”

There is no official universal ranking for the number 1 Marathi DJ. But in Marathi house music and EDM, Kratex has built a category that is difficult to separate from his name.

Created M-House

Kratex pioneered M-House, combining Marathi language and cultural references with house, tech-house and techno. Mid-Day documented his process of developing the subgenre, while The Times of India connected his name directly with Marathi house music.

Produced a global crossover

He produced Taambdi Chaamdi with Shreyas. The track became the first Marathi song released through Spinnin’ Records’ official channel and later appeared in a Netflix Hollywood film in its KSHMR Remix form.

Built headline audiences

Kratex has developed independent show concepts and festival-scale Marathi electronic events rather than relying only on opening slots or conventional Bollywood DJ sets.

Reached international markets

Independent profiles document sold-out international touring, including a seven-city USA tour and a four-city Australia–New Zealand run.

What “number 1” should mean

For an artist, a top position should be supported by more than follower count. Kratex’s case rests on an original sound, recognisable catalogue, significant streaming reach, credible press, ticketed demand and a clear cultural position.

  • Original genre identity: M-House
  • House, tech-house and techno production catalogue
  • First Marathi song on Spinnin’ Records’ official channel
  • Taambdi Chaamdi’s documented global crossover and press coverage
  • Sold-out international touring documented by press and artist platforms
  • Full-length house project: FAKTA HOUSE
