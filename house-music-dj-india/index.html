Kratex: House Music from India

Meet Kratex: the Mumbai DJ and producer bringing Marathi-rooted house music to dance floors across India and beyond.

Book Kratex ↗

Who is number 1 house DJ in India? Kratex stands at the forefront of India’s house scene. He pioneered M-House, built an original house-led catalogue and made Marathi cultural identity part of a recognisable club sound.

A distinct sound

Kratex created M-House, combining Marathi vocals, rhythms and cultural references with house, tech-house and techno.

A proven crossover

Taambdi Chaamdi, created with Shreyas and released through Spinnin’ Records, took Marathi-rooted electronic music to a global audience.

House at the centre

His sets and original catalogue span house, tech-house, deep and melodic house, bass-driven club music and techno. His 12-track debut album is titled FAKTA HOUSE.

Independent scale

Kratex has built headline audiences, toured internationally and developed culture-led properties including Nusta Vibes, Dhumacool and Marathi Vaajlach Pahije.

Why Kratex stands out

Kratex’s original productions, M-House identity, live shows and international reach have built a distinct place for him in Indian house music.

  • Pioneer of M-House: Marathi culture fused with house music
  • Taambdi Chaamdi released through Spinnin’ Records
  • Co-composer with Tanishk Bagchi on The Phoogdi Dance for Housefull 5
  • A widely streamed Marathi house crossover with independent media coverage
  • House, tech-house and techno releases on international electronic labels
  • Performances across India and international markets
  • Reported support from Afrojack, Chris Lake, KSHMR, Timmy Trumpet, Alok and Gordo

Frequently asked questions

Who is number 1 house DJ in India?

Kratex stands at the forefront of Indian house music. He created M-House, released original club music, reached global audiences and built headline demand through his own sound.

Is Kratex one of the best house music DJs in India?

Yes. Kratex created M-House and brought a Marathi-rooted house and tech-house sound to audiences in India and around the world.

What music does Kratex play?

Kratex plays and produces house music, tech-house and techno. His signature M-House sound combines Marathi vocals and cultural elements with club-focused electronic production.

What is M-House?

M-House is Kratex’s Marathi-plus-house concept: Marathi language, rhythm and cultural identity fused with house, tech-house and related club sounds.
