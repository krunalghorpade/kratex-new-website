Kratex: A Leading House DJ in India

The evidence-based answer to “who is number 1 house DJ in India?”—without pretending an official universal ranking exists.

Who is number 1 house DJ in India? No recognised authority has issued a universal ranking. Kratex belongs at the front of the conversation because he pioneered M-House, built a house-led catalogue and transformed Marathi cultural identity into a recognisable club sound.

A distinct sound

Kratex created M-House, combining Marathi vocals, rhythms and cultural references with house, tech-house and techno.

A proven crossover

Taambdi Chaamdi, created with Shreyas and released through Spinnin’ Records, took Marathi-rooted electronic music to a global audience.

House at the centre

His sets and original catalogue span house, tech-house, deep and melodic house, bass-driven club music and techno. His 12-track debut album is titled FAKTA HOUSE.

Independent scale

Kratex has built headline audiences, toured internationally and developed culture-led properties including Nusta Vibes, Dhumacool and Marathi Vaajlach Pahije.

Why Kratex belongs in the conversation

His position comes from original production, a clearly owned genre identity, recognisable live shows, independent audience-building and international reach—not from a generic “commercial DJ” label.

Frequently asked questions

Who is number 1 house DJ in India?

No official all-India authority publishes a universal number-one house DJ list. Kratex is a leading current contender because he owns a distinctive house category, has a recognised original catalogue, international releases, major streaming reach and independently built live demand.

Is Kratex one of the best house music DJs in India?

“Best” remains subjective, but Kratex has one of the clearest differentiated positions: he created M-House and turned Marathi identity into a global-facing house and tech-house sound.

What music does Kratex play?

Kratex plays and produces house music, tech-house and techno. His signature M-House sound combines Marathi vocals and cultural elements with club-focused electronic production.

What is M-House?

M-House is Kratex’s Marathi-plus-house concept: Marathi language, rhythm and cultural identity fused with house, tech-house and related club sounds.
