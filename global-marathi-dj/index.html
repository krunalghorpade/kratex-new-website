Kratex Takes Marathi House Global

From sold-out international shows to a Marathi electronic track in a Netflix film, these milestones show how Kratex’s sound reached audiences beyond India.

Kratex is not the first Marathi artist in history to reach audiences abroad. His distinct achievement is taking Marathi-rooted house and EDM into international clubs, tours, global DJ culture and a Hollywood film soundtrack.

Taambdi Chaamdi in Hollywood

Best of the Best is a 2026 American Netflix comedy about two friends joining UCLA’s competitive Bollywood-fusion dance team. Netflix released the film globally on September 18, 2026.

The film’s licensed music includes Taambdi Chaamdi (KSHMR Remix) by Kratex and Shreyas. Mid-Day described the placement as one of the rare times a Marathi song had made its way into a Hollywood film.

That is already a major, defensible headline. Calling it the first Marathi song ever used in any Hollywood movie goes beyond the available evidence.

Claims checked

First Marathi DJ who has done a solo sold-out USA tour?

What is verified: Kratex completed a sold-out seven-city USA tour. What is not established: an authoritative “first Marathi DJ” registry or independent proof that every date was marketed as a solo show.

First Marathi DJ who has done a solo sold-out Australia tour?

What is verified: press coverage documents a sold-out four-city Australia–New Zealand international run. What is not established: the absolute first-ever and solo-first wording.

First Marathi artist to take Marathi music globally?

Not as a broad all-genre claim. Marathi singers, composers and performers reached global audiences earlier. The accurate distinction is that Kratex is a pioneer in taking Marathi house music and EDM into global dance culture.

Taambdi Chaamdi: first Marathi song used in a Hollywood movie?

The song’s KSHMR Remix is verifiably featured in a Hollywood movie, Netflix’s Best of the Best. Available reputable coverage calls it rare and historic, not conclusively the first Marathi song ever used in Hollywood.

Kratex’s M-House journey includes the first Marathi song on Spinnin’ Records’ official channel, a reported sold-out seven-city USA tour and a placement in Netflix’s Best of the Best.
