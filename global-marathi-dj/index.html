Taambdi Chaamdi in Hollywood

Best of the Best is a 2026 American Netflix comedy about two friends joining UCLA’s competitive Bollywood-fusion dance team. Netflix released the film globally on September 18, 2026.

The film’s licensed music includes Taambdi Chaamdi (KSHMR Remix) by Kratex and Shreyas. Mid-Day described the placement as one of the rare times a Marathi song had made its way into a Hollywood film.

The placement brought Kratex’s Marathi-rooted electronic sound to a global film audience.
